# National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — Official Operational Guidelines

**Issuing Authority**: Ministry of Education (Department of Higher Education) & Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India  
**Official Portal**: [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)  
**Implementation Mechanism**: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) via National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Aadhaar Payment Bridge.

---

## 1. Scheme Overviews

### A. Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (PM-USP CSSS)
- **Objective**: To provide financial assistance to meritorious students from low-income families to meet day-to-day expenses while pursuing higher studies.
- **Target Audience**: College and university students pursuing regular degree/diploma courses in recognized institutions.
- **Quota**: 82,000 fresh scholarships awarded per year (41,000 for boys and 41,000 for girls) divided state-wise based on population in the 18–25 age group.

### B. Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for SC / ST / OBC Students
- **Objective**: To support scheduled caste, scheduled tribe, and other backward class students studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary stages.
- **Coverage**: Class 11, Class 12, ITI, Polytechnic, Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Ph.D. degrees.

### C. Merit-Cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship for Professional and Technical Courses
- **Objective**: To assist meritorious students belonging to economically weaker minority communities to pursue professional and technical courses.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria & Thresholds

| Parameter | Central Sector Scheme (CSSS) | Post-Matric SC/ST | Merit-Cum-Means (MCM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Academic Cut-off** | Above 80th percentile in relevant stream in Class 12 Board Exam | Passed Class 10 / Matriculation | Minimum 50% marks in previous final exam |
| **Annual Family Income Ceiling** | Maximum ₹4,50,000 per annum | Maximum ₹2,50,000 per annum | Maximum ₹2,50,000 per annum |
| **Mode of Study** | Regular / Full-time degree only (Distance / correspondence ineligible) | Regular / recognized course | Regular professional/technical course |
| **Institution Recognition** | Valid AISHE / UDISE code registered on NSP | Recognized state/central institution | Recognized AICTE / UGC institution |
| **Age Limit** | 18 to 25 years at entry | No strict upper age limit | Within prescribed course age |

### Mandatory Exclusions & Restrictions:
1. **No Dual Scholarships**: Beneficiaries receiving any other Central or State Government scholarship or stipend are strictly not eligible.
2. **Renewal Condition**: A minimum of 50% aggregate marks in the previous annual examination and minimum 75% attendance is required for renewal in subsequent years.
3. **Diploma Holders**: Students pursuing diploma courses are not eligible for CSSS unless pursuing an integrated degree.

---

## 3. Financial Benefits & Disbursal Structure

1. **Central Sector Scheme (CSSS)**:
   - **Graduation Level (Years 1, 2, 3)**: ₹12,000 per annum credited via DBT.
   - **Post-Graduation Level (Years 4, 5)**: ₹20,000 per annum.
   - **5-Year Integrated Courses**: ₹12,000/year for first 3 years, and ₹20,000/year for 4th and 5th year.
2. **Post-Matric Scheme**:
   - **Compulsory Non-Refundable Fees**: Complete tuition fee reimbursement as approved by the State Fee Regulatory Committee.
   - **Maintenance Allowance**: Up to ₹1,200 per month for hostellers and ₹550 per month for day scholars.
3. **Merit-cum-Means (MCM)**:
   - **Course Fee**: ₹20,000 per annum or actual fee (whichever is less).
   - **Maintenance Allowance**: ₹1,000 per month for hostellers (10 months) / ₹500 per month for day scholars.

## 4. Mandatory Documentation Requirements

All applicants must possess and verify the following official documents:
1. **Aadhaar Card (UIDAI)**: Mandatory for One Time Registration (OTR) and identity verification. Minors may use parent's Aadhaar.
2. **Income Certificate**: Issued by a competent revenue authority not below the rank of Tehsildar or Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), valid for the current financial year.
3. **Caste / Community Certificate**: Issued by the authorized state revenue department (for SC, ST, OBC, or Minority applicants).
4. **Previous Examination Marksheet**: Class 10 and Class 12 board marksheet or previous semester marksheet.
5. **Bonafide Student Certificate**: Issued by the Head of the Institution / Registrar confirming regular enrollment and AISHE code.
6. **Bank Account Details**: Active savings bank account seeded with Aadhaar and mapped to the NPCI mapper for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Joint accounts or minor accounts without full KYC are not permitted.

## 5. Official Application Procedure (Step-by-Step)

- **Step 1: One Time Registration (OTR)**:
  - Download NSP OTR App or visit `scholarships.gov.in`.
  - Perform Aadhaar Face Authentication or OTP e-KYC.
  - A unique 14-digit OTR reference number is generated.
- **Step 2: Profile & Academic Application**:
  - Login using OTR credentials.
  - Select Domicile State, Scholarship Category (Post-Matric / Top-Class), and Institution AISHE code.
  - Enter marks, stream, roll number, and family annual income.
- **Step 3: Document Upload & Verification**:
  - Upload income certificate, caste certificate, and bonafide certificate.
- **Step 4: Two-Level Institutional & State Verification (L1 / L2)**:
  - Level 1: Institute Nodal Officer (INO) verifies student bonafide status.
  - Level 2: District Nodal Officer (DNO) or State Nodal Officer (SNO) validates domicile and income credentials.
- **Step 5: Merit List Generation & DBT Disbursal**:
  - Central Ministry releases merit list; PFMS initiates payment directly to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
